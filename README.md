---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Solved

* **Which category drives the revenue?** Compares Laptop, Phone, and Headphone sales in a donut/pie chart to show each category's share.
* **Which products sell the most?** Ranks all six products (Dell XPS 13, iPhone 11 Pro, OnePlus 8 Pro, HP Envy x360, OnePlus Buds, Apple AirPods Pro) by total sales.
* **Where is geographic demand strongest?** Compares Bangalore, Cochin, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Mumbai to spot high-performing and under-performing markets.
* **Who are the top-performing sales reps?** Ranks 10 reps (Amar, Kate, Tara, Aryan, and others) by total sales amount.
* **How do sales change over time?** Tracks monthly revenue from January to December to identify seasonal peaks and dips.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methods

* **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Prep:** Power Query (data cleaning, type casting, handling blanks)
* **Data Modeling:** Relational model with DAX measures (Sum of Amount)
* **Visualization Formats:** Pie chart, line chart, column chart, horizontal bar charts, and slicers

---

## 💡 Strategic Takeaways

1. **Double Down on Laptops:** Laptops generate the largest share of revenue, and the Dell XPS 13 alone outsells every other product, so stock and marketing focus should stay here.
2. **Fix the Cochin Gap:** Cochin sits clearly below Bangalore, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Mumbai, which points to an opportunity for local campaigns, offers, or more sales coverage.
3. **Plan Around Seasonal Peaks:** Revenue is stable through the year, but April, July, October, and December peak, so promotions and inventory should be timed to these months.
4. **Support Lower-Performing Reps:** Sales reps at the bottom (such as Laxmi at 0.6M) could benefit from coaching, better territory allocation, or realistic targets.
